We found this race last October, three days into our rotation in Munich, and booked our tickets before we had even worked out where the good bakery was. It was the first plan we made for our time abroad, and it is still the one we have been most excited about. Now we've flown to Brussels, the board is up, the shirt is loud, and we are about to start our first Lupine race.

What we've signed up for

The idea
A self-organised public transport race across Europe. 200 racers, one start, no set route.
The catch
Checkpoints are handed out in a race pack on the start line — this year themed on 20th-century European history.
The clock
Brussels 11:00 Sunday to Ljubljana by 16:00 Saturday, then the awards party at Ljubljana Castle.
The prizes
Fastest overall, most countries collected, and lowest spend on long-distance travel.
