Enough contrast yet?

The shipped cascade separates its cards from the ground by 1.25 : 1. Before the dark-mode change, cream on espresso separated them by 16.45 : 1 — so moving off cream gave up all of it and nothing took over the job. Fill alone cannot fix it: two steps brighter is the ceiling before the muted ink drops under 4.5 : 1. The middle frame spends the whole budget on fill so you can see how far that gets on its own; the right-hand frame splits it between fill and edge.

All three are shown at the same theme, so the only variable is the card. The ratios under each frame are measured from the rendered pixels and follow the switch.

What changes in code. A third ground step, --bg-3 (#423225 dark, #FFFFFF light — the light ladder tops out at white, so light gets its improvement from the edge instead). Then four values repoint in the tone block the last change already added, and cascade cards gain an inset top highlight from --card-emboss, which already exists per theme. Re-pointing the hairline to --line-strong is what firms up the rows and fields inside the cards too — the other half of "not enough contrast". The scorepad is untouched by all of it: the paper island restates its own hairline, so the stronger edge cannot leak in. Flip to Light — the fill is identical across all three there, and only the edge moves, which is the honest picture of what light gets out of this.
