Which tone should the cascade cards take?

Gather, Play and Settle Up currently paint with the paper tokens, so every card is cream in dark mode. Both options move them onto the app's ground. They differ by exactly one declaration — the card's background token — and everything else in the change is identical either way. The scoring grid stays a cream scorepad and the parchment scroll is untouched, as decided.

Both options are shown at the same theme so the difference is the tone, not the mode.

What is the same in both. Cards move off --polaroid-* onto the ground tokens; player rows, the notes field and the photo dropzone become --well; the "Gather / Play / Settle Up" heading stops being var(--polaroid-bg), which renders it white-on-cream at about 1.05:1 in light mode today; the Continue button moves from a literal #fff to --on-accent; card shadows move off hardcoded black onto the shadow ramp; and the co-op outcome buttons stop being near-black blocks on the scorepad. Flip the switch above to Light to check the second theme — the difference between A and B is nearly imperceptible there, which is the point.

Decided: B, Lamplit. Shipped as a small tone block layered over the shared chrome re-point rather than a second copy of it, so the cascade still inherits every other value from one place. .player-picker-sheet and .game-search-sheet move with it because Gather is their only call site; .game-picker-sheet (Stats) and .exp-picker (Collection) stay at --bg-1.
