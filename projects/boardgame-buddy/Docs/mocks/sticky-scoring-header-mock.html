Picking up the column headers

The scoring grid used to be a bounded pane — max-height plus overflow: auto — which is what let its header row pin. The cost was two competing scrolls: a pane scroll sitting inside the page scroll, with a flick landing on whichever one the thumb happened to start over.

Here the grid is fully expanded and there is one scroll. The column headers are picked up at the point they would leave the top of the screen, hold there, and are handed back at the last round row — so they can never cover the Total row.

How the release is built rather than tuned

A sticky box is bounded by its containing block. Put the Total row outside the header's containing block and the release point is structural — there is no measurement to drift.

.rg__head

The pin. position: sticky at top: var(--rg-pin-top), which is the global header's height token — and 0px on the tiers that fold the header into a side rail, with no tier rule of its own.

.rg__pinzone

The release. Wraps the header and the round rows, and nothing else. Its bottom edge is the last round row, so that is where the header is handed back.

.rg__foot

Out of reach. The Total row lives outside the pin zone, so no scroll position exists at which the header overlaps it. It also loses its old bottom pin.

.rg__body

Sideways still works. The one horizontal scroller. All three regions share a <colgroup> and mirror scrollLeft, so they cannot fall out of column.

What to check before this ships:
  • At 3 rounds the grid is shorter than the screen and the header should never pin at all — nothing to pick up.
  • At 25 rounds it should pin for the whole run and be pushed off, not snapped off, as Total arrives.
  • At 6 players the body scrolls sideways; the header and the Total row must travel with it, and the round-label column must stay pinned on the left in all three.
  • Flip to Name headers: the header band grows and the pin offset has to follow it (it is a token, --rg-head-h, which is why the new-row scroll still clears it).
  • Both themes: the pinned band must be opaque, or the rows ghost through it.
  • The step header pins under the global header and the grid's band under both, so Back and End session stay one tap away however far down the grid you are. Once the grid band is released it slides behind the step header — z 15 under z 20.
  • Known cosmetic: on a desktop with classic scrollbars the body's horizontal scrollbar sits between the last round row and Total. On touch it is an overlay and takes no space.
