One app, three widths

Every feature stays exactly where it is. What changes is how the same data is laid out once the screen stops being a phone: a wider column on an iPad held upright, and a left rail with room for two panes once it is turned sideways or opened on a monitor. Nothing below is a new screen; every frame is a screen the app already has.

Phone < 768px
Today's layout, byte for byte. Bottom bar, 480px column, rails inline in the feed.
Tablet 768 – 1023px
iPad held upright. Column widens to 720px; the bottom bar stays because thumbs are still at the bottom. Screens that split do so here.
Wide ≥ 1024px
iPad sideways, laptop, monitor. The bar becomes an 88px left rail carrying the logo, the three tabs, Alerts and Settings; the header folds into it. Column up to 1040px.
01Feed decide: sidebar widths

Play cards are photographs and should not stretch, so the stream keeps one column. The two rail cards — Hot this week and Buddies you may know — leave the stream and stand in a sticky sidebar where they are always in reach. Tablet gives the sidebar 220px (the stream stays as wide as a phone's); wide gives it 300px beside a 560px stream.

02Play step — the scoring table decide: guide pane at 38%

The screen a tablet is actually propped up for. The scoring grid takes the left pane — six players fit without sideways scrolling — and the reference guide stands beside it in a sticky pane that scrolls on its own, so the host reads the end-of-game scoring while filling in the last round. Wrap up docks under the grid, exactly the width of the pane it belongs to. The spectator mirror gets the same split.

03Gather and Settle Up

Same two-pane frame as the Play step so the cascade never changes shape between steps. Gather puts the session code, the game, the mode and the expansions on the left and the players on the right, where the roster can grow without pushing anything down. Settle Up keeps the date and place on the left and gives the photo the right pane; key-moment notes span the full width underneath.

04Play tab — Host beside Join

The tab is already two halves. Side by side, hosting and joining read as the two ways into a game rather than one list scrolling into another, and the live-session list is visible without scrolling past the host cards.

05Collection grid and game detail

Polaroid grids go from three columns to four on a tablet and six on a monitor; the tile itself never changes. Game detail puts the box-art polaroid, the status pill and the actions in a sticky left column and lets the description, expansions, reference scroll and recent plays run down the right.

06Settings — the Layout control

The tier follows the screen width by default. A new Layout control sits directly under Theme and works the same way: Auto follows the device, Phone or Tablet pins it. A pinned Tablet never becomes the wide rail, and never applies below 600px, so a real phone cannot be pushed into two panes. Reading screens like this one keep a single 720px column even on a monitor.
